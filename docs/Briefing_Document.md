## Competition Overview

**Competitor Materials:** DSMLC Datathon 2026 Materials

The Data Science and Machine Learning Club at the University of Calgary is hosting a three-day oil and gas data competition. It’s open to students at any Calgary university and provides students an opportunity to work with real-world problems, utilize industry tools and develop knowledge and connections within the energy industry.

The datathon is proudly sponsored by Chirality Research and Canary Labs, in partnership with dataset provider, Strathcona Resources and data/cloud sponsor Databricks. Through these collaborations, participants will have the opportunity to work with industry-provided datasets, explore modern data platforms, and develop innovative solutions to challenges facing the oil and gas industry.

Participants will earn recognition for their work, which helps them build a portfolio and grow a career in analytics, programming and data science. Above all, the competition aims to build community among Calgary’s university students, help participants gain hands-on experience and make connections that can help them reach their career goals.

## Competition Rules

1. **Team Eligibility:** The competition is open to all students attending a Calgary post-secondary institution. Teams must have 2–4 members; you can’t participate as a solo team. Teams must register in the official Datathon Discord to officially join.
2. **Originality and Attribution:** Teams must create reproducible results, produce original work within the competition time frame and have contributions made to their project by registered team members only.
3. **Project Ownership:** Teams retain rights to their original work. Participation doesn’t grant rights to redistribute restricted data.
4. **Disqualification:** A team may be disqualified from the competition without further notice if the team is suspected of cheating, exhibits toxic behaviour or fails to follow any of the rules.
5. **Communication:** All participants are expected and encouraged to join the competition Discord for all updates, organizer communication and for team registration.

## Expectations & Deliverables

Competitors must form teams of 2–4 and work with a real-world time series case provided by Chirality Research & Strathcona Resources. Each team is expected to do the following:

1. **Explore the Data:** Understand the relationships, patterns, limitations and available data.
2. **Identify a Problem:** Discover a challenge or opportunity that is supported by the dataset, context and background research.
3. **Develop a Research Question:** Define a specific and measurable outcome that the project will investigate and develop their solution around.
4. **Create/Propose a Solution:** Apply data science techniques, machine learning, statistical analysis and domain knowledge to develop an AI/ML application, dashboard or implementation plan.
5. **Validate Findings:** Demonstrate why their results are supported by evidence. Acknowledge their own pitfalls, limitations and explore future development ideas.
6. **Communicate:** Explain the project’s analysis, solution and relevance.

Each team develops a solution in one of three forms:

1. **An analytical dashboard:** An interactive view that turns raw time series into clear visuals, key metrics and trends, so operators can track system performance at a glance and spot problems early.
2. **A prototype AI/ML application:** A working tool that applies machine learning to the data, such as an autonomous control system, a real-time monitoring service or an anomaly detection model that flags unusual behaviour before it becomes a costly failure.
3. **An implementation or recommendation plan:** A data-driven strategy that pinpoints opportunities in the case, proposes concrete solutions and lays out how to deploy them, backed by analysis, research and industry context.

Each team submits three deliverables: a presentation of their analysis and research, a 5-minute summary video and their source code.

The top projects are invited to present on stage, where finalists compete for a podium finish and prizes on November 22nd.

## Problem Statement

**Dataset:** Competition Dataset

The oil and gas industry generates vast amounts of time-series data through production processes, industrial sensors, and operational equipment. These datasets provide valuable insights into production performance, equipment conditions, and operational activities, creating opportunities for identifying inefficiencies, detecting anomalies, forecasting trends, and optimizing industrial processes.

### The Challenge

**Theme: Optimization**

You are a team of data consultants hired by Strathcona Resources to identify opportunities for operational optimization. They provide 5 years of data for:

- Daily Production Data by Well
- Production Failure Records
- Maintenance Activities

Using the provided datasets, investigate the company's operations, develop your own research question, and propose a data-driven solution that delivers measurable business or operational value.

## Presentations & Judging Criteria

There will be two judging stages, the preliminary judging and final presentation judging. Only finalists are eligible for any awards.

1. **Preliminary Judging:** Teams will submit a 5-minute project summary video outlining their analysis, research and solution. The team's code, documentation and slides will also be judged at this stage. The best projects and presentations from this preliminary stage are selected as finalists and will present on the final stage.
2. **Finalist Judging:** On Nov 22nd at 1:30 PM, finalists will be announced one-by-one on to the stage. Each finalist team will have 10 minutes to present along with 3 minutes of Q&A. The following rubric will be provided to the judges to determine the winners from the finalist group.
