## Problem Statement

**Turn upstream time-series data into industrial intelligence.**

Oil and gas operations produce a constant stream of production, sensor and equipment data. Use the historical series to build something operators could actually use: spot inefficiencies, flag anomalies, forecast trends or optimize the process.

## Strathcona Resources Case: The Challenge

You are a team of data consultants hired by Strathcona Resources to identify opportunities for operational optimization. They provide 5 years of data for:

1. Daily Production Data by Well
2. Production Failure Records
3. Maintenance Activities

Using the provided datasets, investigate the company's operations, develop your own research question, and propose a data-driven solution that delivers measurable business or operational value.

## The Judging Rubric

- **Research Depth & Domain Expertise:** Science, engineering and industry ideas beyond the basics
- **Technical Implementation & Correctness:** Does it work, is it justified, does it scale?
- **Insight & Business Impact:** Feasible and valuable for industry
- **Presentation & Communication:** Clear, convincing and on time
- **Creativity & Innovation:** A fresh, thoughtful approach

## Tips for Success: How to Stand Out

1. **Research first.** Understand the problem before you build. Learn how the wells and the data really work.
2. **Leverage AI & diverse teams.** Use AI tools, and team up with people from different majors and backgrounds.
3. **Practice. Practice. Practice.** Rehearse your pitch. Be concise and easy to follow, and know the tech stack beneath it.
4. **Be yourself.** Have fun, learn something new and take something positive away.
