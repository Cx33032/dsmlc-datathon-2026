# Datathon: Research Topic Guide

**Chirality Research & Canary Labs**
Data Science and Machine Learning Club, November 2026

## Challenge

**Theme: Optimization**

Operations, asset maintenance, finance and costs, anomaly detection, and production are just some of the areas you can explore when developing your optimization research question.

Our advice: Approach the dataset from the perspective of a data consultant contracted by an oil and gas company to identify opportunities for improvement. What problems can you uncover through the data, and how can you develop a solution that delivers measurable business or operational value?

We encourage you to think critically, explore different approaches, and develop your own research question based on the insights you discover.

There is no single correct solution. Your goal is to demonstrate how data-driven decision-making can improve efficiency, reduce costs, mitigate risks, or optimize performance in a real-world industrial setting.

Everyone has the same case, what makes your team stand out?

## Developing a Research Question

Start by identifying a broad area of interest within the dataset. From there, consider what factors, patterns, or relationships you want to investigate. Use these observations to develop a focused research question that addresses a meaningful optimization opportunity.

Remember: A strong research question should be specific, data-driven, and relevant to real-world operational challenges.

## Potential Research Areas

The following research areas are intended to inspire your team, not define your final research question. You are encouraged to explore beyond these examples, identify your own problems, and develop original approaches to optimization.

- **Production Performance**
  What explains production differences across wells, and how can these inform operational improvements?
- **Predictive Maintenance**
  Can historical production patterns identify wells at increased risk of equipment failure?
- **Downtime and Production Losses**
  What contributes most to production losses, and how can operators prioritize downtime reduction?
- **Maintenance Effectiveness**
  How does well performance change after maintenance, and which interventions deliver the greatest benefit?
- **Operational Optimization**
  How do operating parameters relate to production outcomes, and how can they inform efficiency improvements?
- **Well Reliability**
  Can recurring failure patterns across wells or equipment improve maintenance planning?
- **Intelligent Decision Support**
  How can operational data be combined to identify wells requiring urgent attention?
- **Industrial Data Quality**
  How do missing, inconsistent, or abnormal records affect production analytics and operational decisions?
